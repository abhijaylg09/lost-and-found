Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions

How do I claim a found item?

Browse the directory, find a match, and click "View Details" to see the contact information. Reach out to the finder directly to verify and arrange pickup.

Is my personal info safe?

Absolutely. Your contact details are only shown to authenticated users viewing item details. We never share data with third parties.

How long do listings stay active?

Items remain listed until the poster marks them as resolved. We recommend updating or closing your post once the item is recovered.

Do I need an account?

You can browse items without one. To post a report or contact a finder, creating a free account takes less than a minute.
